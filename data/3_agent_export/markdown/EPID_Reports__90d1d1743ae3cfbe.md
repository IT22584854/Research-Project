Title: Weekly Epidemiological Report

## Weekly Epidemiological Report
It is a record which disseminates epidemiological information on communicable diseases and outbreaks which has public health importance. A district summary of notified and confirmed communicable diseases is published and provides feedback to the communicable disease notification system. It includes a leading article on a timely and important topic of the country. It is prepared and disseminated weekly to all levels of the health system up to the divisional level.[ Read More](https://www.epid.gov.lk/storage/post/pdfs/en_63ec87e11b430_WER.pdf)
