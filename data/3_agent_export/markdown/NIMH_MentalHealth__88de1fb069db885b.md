## General Guidelines
#### Read the following guidelines before filling the assesment form.
  * Navodaya is a Day Service providing psychological treatments & specialized day services.
  * Since clinical assessment has to be done, only general bookings can be accommodated via online.
  * The service will not allow immediate bookings but with a **3 working day gap** (Saturday & Sunday not included).
  * This is only for booking and for **emergencies** to come to **NIMH or nearest hospital**.
  * Followinf form has to be filled for all sessions even for repeat patients as mental states can change.
  * The Navodaya team will assess the form and contact the patient and confirm your booking.
