## OPD
The Largest Hospital for Mental Health in Sri Lanka
October 3, 2017 / by / 
###  **Opening Hours** : 24 Hours
**Facilities** :
  * 24 Hour Out Patient Department
  * 24 Hour Psychiatric Intensive Care Unit for managing aggressive patients
  * Referral to Psychiatric Clinics if admission not needed


**Important** :
  * Patients are admitted according to their permanent address.
  * If the patient hasn’t a permanent address, he or she is admitted on rotation wise method.
  * Patients can come on their own or can be brought by their families, by police, by prison or other non-relatives.
  * Patients are assessed by admission doctor with a nursing officer from the relevant admission ward.
