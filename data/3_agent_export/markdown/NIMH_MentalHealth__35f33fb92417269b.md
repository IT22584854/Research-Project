## Community Psychiatry Programme
The Largest Hospital for Mental Health in Sri Lanka
October 3, 2017 / by / 
### **Opening Hours** : Mon – Fri (8am-4pm) & Sat (8am-12pm)
**Facilities** :
  * Detecting new patients in the community.
  * Following up discharged patients in the community.
  * Finding patients who have stopped treatment in the community.
  * Providing intra-muscular depot injections for those patients who do not take medication in the community.
  * Solving social and legal problems of the patient in the community.


**Important** :
  * New patients referred by the Community Psychiatric Nurse, Medical Officer (Mental Health), Public Health Midwife or Gramma Niladari is also visited by our team.
  * Depending on the case they are either admitted or provided community based treatment as advised by the Consultant Psychiatrist.
  * **Outreach Clinics:** Please [click here to see the locations and timings of our outreach clinics](https://nimh.health.gov.lk/en/clinic-times/).
