## Dental Unit
The Largest Hospital for Mental Health in Sri Lanka
October 3, 2017 / by / 
### **Opening Hours** : Mon – Fri (8am-4pm) & Sat (8am-12pm)
**Facilities** :
  * Oral care is integrated as part of the overall management.
  * Dental care for Out Patients and In Ward Patients.
  * General dental checkup.
  * Correction of median diestema.
  * Corrections of discoloration of teeth.
  * Correction of irregularities of teeth.
