# Vision and Mission
[Home](https://lrh.health.gov.lk/) /  Vision and Mission
**Our Vision** is to be one of the best centers for PEDIATRIC HEALTH CARE in the region
**Our Mission** is to provide the best and most comprehensive paediatric care for all children who are referred to us from all over Sri Lanka & neighbouring countries. Our entire staff is dedicated to provide child friendly, best quality and safe healthcare with individualized personal attention. With the guidance of Ministry of Health, and collaboration with all donors and other stakeholders our endeavor is to achieve expected standards with continued training of our staff.
**Our Values**
  * **Quality and Safety**. We maintain the highest standards and achieve them by continually measuring and improving our outcomes.
  * **Innovation**. We welcome change, encourage invention and continually seek better, more efficient ways to achieve our goals.
  * **Teamwork**. We collaborate and share knowledge to benefit patients and fellow caregivers for the advancement of our mission.
  * **Service**. We strive to exceed our patients’ and/or fellow caregivers’ expectations for comfort and convenience.
  * **Integrity**. We adhere to high moral principles and professional standards by a commitment to honesty, confidentiality, trust, respect and transparency.
