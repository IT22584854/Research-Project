# **Other Services**
**1.)****Mithuru Piyasa**
**Mission**
To give the best help in the hour of need.
**Vision**
To become the best functioning " Mituru Piyasa " in Sri Lanka.
**Services**
1). To give the psychological help to the clients seeking help.
2). To coordinate help with other institutions and organizations and provide the best services to the client.
3). Establish the family support.
4). Awareness about the unit among staff and the public.
5). To provide the befriending services.
