######  Overview 
The sections strategic information, monitoring and evaluation are linked. Hence the activities are carried out synchronizing with each other. The e-HEAPMIS is the electronic Health Education & Health Promotion Management Information System of HPB, which is functional through the e-RHMIS system of Family Health Bureau. The data on HE & HP activities originate from Medical Officer of Health level from the H1282 format monthly. The activities carried out by the Health Education Officers and other district level health officials within HE & HP are to be entered quarterly to H1283 format from district level. The data from these two main implementing arms can be visualized at each level by respective points and overall at national level in HPB. 
The information generated via e-HEAPMIS can be triangulated and validated at the quarterly HE & HP reviews held nationally by the monitoring and evaluation section of the Strategic Information, Planning, Monitoring & Evaluation Unit. The HEOO of the districts, Municipal Councils of Colombo & Kandy, NSACP, NPTCCD and HPB present in the reviews and get constructive feedback from the technical units of HPB for further strengthening of their functions.

#### National HEO Review
2 Day National Level Health Education and Health Promotion Mid Year review 2024 (2024.08.27 & 2024.08.28) Held at Hector Kobbekaduwa Agrarian research and training institute Colombo 7. Health Education and Health promotion activities done at district level during the first 6 months of the year were presented by the health education officers.
