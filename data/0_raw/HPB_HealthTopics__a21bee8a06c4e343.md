#### behavior research unit activity
HPB prioritizes research in behavior change and health promotion by identifying key needs and gaps in the field. We aim to establish and maintain a comprehensive research database conducted on these topics. Additionally, we foster collaborative research initiatives with local and international partner universities to enhance the effectiveness of health promotion efforts. 
#### ParentText Initiative
Establish and sustain the innovative mobile messaging project, 'ParentText,' to enhance parenting practices. The goal is to create a scalable foundation for a hybrid, engaging curriculum tailored for Sri Lanka. This project will involve adapting and developing a 5-day user experience with optional courses for parents of children aged 2-9. The project will be a collaborative effort between the Health Promotion Bureau (HPB) and Oxford University (USA), which will work together to design and implement this impactful initiative.
