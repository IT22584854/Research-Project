# Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children
Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children is the largest free of charge Pediatric Hospital in Sri Lanka. It serves as the national referral center for pediatric care for Sri Lanka. Once deemed to be the largest Children’s Hospital in South Asia, Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children (LRH) is working with a vision of becoming the best health care center for children in South Asia by 2025.
# **OUR VISION**
To be one of the best centers for PEDIATRIC HEALTH CARE in the region
# **OUR MISSION**
To provide the best and most comprehensive paediatric care for all children who are referred to us from all over Sri Lanka & neighbouring countries. Our entire staff is dedicated to provide child friendly, best quality and safe healthcare with individualized personal attention. With the guidance of Ministry of Health, and collaboration with all donors and other stakeholders our endeavor is to achieve expected standards with continued training of our staff.
# Director’s Message
As a healthcare worker, I am proud and thrilled to be a part of Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children (LRH). It is a loving home dedicated to the wellbeing of children of this nation. Having a legacy of 125 years of excellence which provides a strong foundation, we look forward to the future with a lot of hope and dreams of expanding our services. We our focused on improving the quality of our health services offered. We at LRH pledge to continue to work with dedication for the physical and mental well-being of the country’s little children.
_– Dr. K. D. Pradeep Wijesinghe MBBS MSc(Med. Admin) Msc(Health Service Management) MD(Med. Admin)_

# Flashback from History…
Lady Ridgeway Hospital is a gift from the colloquial rule of the English. During the period 1890-1895 when Sir Arthur Havelock was Governor of Ceylon, one of his greatest achievements was making available many advances and new opportunities in medical science to the island’s inhabitants.
