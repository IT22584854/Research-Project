# Monitoring & Evaluation
  * [Strategic Information, Planning, Monitoring & Evaluation Unit](https://hpb.health.gov.lk/technical-unit/strategic-information-planning-monitoring-evaluation-unit/)
  * Monitoring & Evaluation


######  Overview 
HE & HP reviews are held nationally by the monitoring and evaluation section of the Strategic Information, Planning, Monitoring & Evaluation Unit. Reviews are held quarterly and annually. 
The HEOO of the districts, Municipal Councils of Colombo & Kandy, NSACP, NPTCCD and HPB, present the district and organizational performance in the HE & HP reviews and get constructive feedback from the technical units of HPB on the progress of achieving the HE&HP targets to further strengthen the district progress.
