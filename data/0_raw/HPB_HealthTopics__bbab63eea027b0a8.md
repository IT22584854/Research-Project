###  Welcome to HPB
Health Promotion Bureau (HPB) is the national centre of excellence for health communication, health promotion, media and publicity in the Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka. Improving health, wellbeing and quality of life of people by empowering them to take control over the determinants of their health through advocacy, mediation and enabling is the main goal of HPB. In this path mobilizing and empowering communities and settings are emphasized.All functions of the HPB are carried out by seven technical units and administrative section nationally, and through a network of provincial and district level Health Education Officers, other health and health related stakeholders across the country.

### Director’s Message
Behavior change is the cornerstone of prevention, management and damage control of many adverse health events and outcomes of modern time. Be it antibiotic resistance, mental health issues, non-communicable diseases or communicable diseases, behavior change of individuals, families and communities will deliver the best wellbeing return for the investment. In the context of competing priorities in investment for health, let alone the total investment for public health, health promotion should be the priority considering the stable and long-term return on investment. Whereas countries which took early steps in funding health promotion are reaping the benefits, it is never too late to make the first steps. Communication for mediation, enabling, establishment of supportive settings and platforms through media and publicity is priority thrust area for health promotion. Community mobilization and engagement through risk communication in modern days of social media presence and activism is an uphill task to undertake for all health promoters. Advocacy: health communication for policy change and targeted communication for change of behavior in relation to determinants of health encompasses the diversity and the range of baseline communications for health promotion. Community engagement and empowerment achieved and sustained through co-creation and accountability, underlines the Health Promotion Bureau approach to achieve its goal of sustainable healthy and happy living for all Sri Lankans. Lets be health promotion conscious in all our activities, on a daily basis.
###### Dr. Ranjith Batuwanthudawe
Director, Health Promotion Bureau, Ministry of health, Sri Lanka.

Annual Preschool Health Promotion Review
The Preschool Health Promotion Setting is one of the 5 settings focused for health promotion by the Health Promotion Bureau. The progress of preschool health promotion settings development program is reviewed annually by the Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation unit. The key stakeholders representing health, early childhood development and preschool settings from provincial, district and divisional levels gather at the review to present the progress based on three key areas of evaluation: establishment, functionality and multi-sector engagement in implementation of preschool health promotion program. While progress is reviewed, many success stories and experiences are shared for wider learning. This year the annual preschool health promotion progress reviews started from 26th November 2024 where Western and Southern Provinces were reviewed. On 29th November Central, Uva and Sabaragamuwa Provinces, on 03rd December the North Western and North Central Provinces and on 10th December Northern and Eastern Provinces were reviewed. All officials from the different sectors were also appreciated by awarding a certificate of appreciation. UNICEF is the sole funding agency for this program.
