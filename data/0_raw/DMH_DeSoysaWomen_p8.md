# **Vision & Mission**
**Vision**
To be the center of excellence in women health care in Sri Lanka.
**Mission**
To be the center of excellence in maternal, gynaecological & neonatal care by providing quality health care with available resources in a friendly environmental for both workers and patients, while training health professionals related to the field and doing ethical research to upgrade the care and service.
