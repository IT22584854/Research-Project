# Laboratory Animal Sciences
#### **Staff of the Department of Laboratory Animal Sciences**
## **Sub-units of the Animal Center**
The animal center comprises units such as animal breeding, infectious animal experiments,noninfectious animal experiments, laboratory animal feed production, and large animal areas.The animal breeding area is controlled by a barrier system to prevent infections coming in andnucleus breeding colonies of laboratory animals are maintained in this area. Laboratory animalfeed is produced in the feed production area and in the large animal area sheep are beingmaintained for blood drawing purposes laboratory media preparation and various otherdiagnostic purposes.
