The Largest Hospital for Mental Health in Sri Lanka

### **Opening Hours** : Mon – Fri (8am-4pm) & Sat (8am-12pm)
**Facilities** :
  * Has been established to give outside clients psychiatric services.
  * Application of psycho-therapeutic techniques for minor mental health problems.
  * Support other mental health disorders.
  * Engaging in preventive health via education on mental health and anti-narcotics workshops in the community.
  * Support groups for clients battling alcohol addiction. **Alcohol & Narcotics Eradication Programme: Mon 2pm – 4pm.**
