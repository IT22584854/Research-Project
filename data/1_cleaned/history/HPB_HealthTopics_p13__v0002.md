#  Unit Overview 
The Community Health Promotion and Social Media Unit of the Health Promotion Bureau (HPB) is dedicated to improving the health of Sri Lankans through community-based initiatives. We are pioneers in grassroots-level health promotion, emphasizing bottom-up interventions with true community engagement. Our effectiveness is reflected in international collaborations, including the INPARD project with Oxford University and ongoing research with Imperial College London on the Happy Village Plus Initiative. Over the years, we have developed diverse educational tools, from traditional interactive materials to digital gamified health education to meet the public’s evolving needs. The unit also plays a key role in the training of trainers capacity for health promotion. Additionally, we actively engage in collaborative research on health promotion and communication to strengthen evidence-based practices.
Main Functions: 1. Community Health Promotion: Lead grassroots health promotion initiatives with active community engagement (e.g., Happy Village Programme and Happy Village Plus Initiative). 2. Health Communication: Utilize social media platforms for timely, impactful health messaging. Create and disseminate tailored health messages for the diverse Sri Lankan population. 3. Innovative Tools Development: Develop gamified and digital interactive health education materials. Promote NCD prevention through targeted interventions and campaigns. 4 .Training and Capacity Building: Conduct postgraduate training and Training of Trainers (TOT) programs on health promotion, community mobilization, and social media health communication. 5. Research and Collaboration: Advance evidence-based health promotion strategies through research, including international collaborations. 6. Technical Advisory: Provide expert support for NCD and cancer prevention initiatives.
