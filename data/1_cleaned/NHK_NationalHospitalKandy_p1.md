## Our Vision & Mission
Our Vision - To be the safest tertiary care hospital in Sri Lanka. 
Our Mission - Our mission is to elevate the status of the hospital to a modern, leading health care facility and training center by developing and sustaining the infrastructure and human resources and by creating a patient friendly environment where patient care needs will be of highest priority.

The National Hospital Kandy is the second largest medical institution in Sri Lanka.
### Welcome to The National Hospital Kandy
Established and administered under the purview of the Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka which is a key Government Hospital maintained by the Sri Lankan Government. We mainly serve general public living in the Central Province which consists of Kandy, Matale and Nuwara Eliya districts. 
##### 24/7 Service
We provides 24/7 emergency services that are reliable, efficient and effective. 
##### Top Level Staff
NHK has a varied panel of consultants to cater to your specialist needs. 
##### Qualified Facilities
NHK has 75 wards for in-patients. It also has 2741 beds and 11 Operating theaters. 
##### Modern Equipment
NHK has 11 modern operating theatre and modern radiology facilities, Laboratories and 12 Intensive care units. 
##### Professional Services
Our objective is to provide the highest quality patient care to our clients using existing facilities. 
##### Emergency Help
Our emergency teams have the expertise and experience to deliver any type of medical emergency.
