#  FAQ
How can I get information about Clinic Services?
You can contact the Hospital Information Centre on 1959 or by going into Clinic services search page 
[Click Clinic services](https://www.nhsl.health.gov.lk/web/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=8&Itemid=237)
How to find a place in hospital where I need to go?
You can contact the Hospital Information Centre on 1959 or by going into the Route Map
[Click Hospital Map](https://www.nhsl.health.gov.lk/web/index.php?Itemid=234&id=73&option=com_content&view=article&lang=ta)
What are the time periods the Hospital OPD Operates?
You can call the Hospital Information Centre on 1959 or by going into OPD Services

You can contact the Hospital Information Center on 1959
How to find details of a patient admitted to the accident service?
How to find details in managing a poisoning?
You can call the hospital information centre on 1959 or National Poison Centre at NHSL on 0112686143

You can call the Hospital information centre on 1959.

You can call the Hospital information centre during 8.00am to 4pm or the relevant medical/ surgical unit or the hospital exchange by dialling 1959.

What is Clinical Neurophysiology?
It is diagnostic speciality which has evolved from neurology. It is mainly focused on recording and clinically interpreting electrical activity from the brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves and muscles to diagnose disorders of the central and peripheral nerves systems.
Clinical Neurophysiologist is a specialized consultant neurophysician who performs electro diagnostic tests; interpret results in a correct clinical context providing accurate diagnoses.
